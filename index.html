(01) — About

We build interfaces that feel like objects — not documents.

DevOPs3D is a small studio working at the seam of real-time graphics and production infrastructure. We prototype in shaders, ship on pipelines, and treat performance as a design constraint rather than an afterthought.

Every surface you see here is rendered on the GPU, scrubbed against scroll position, and deployed from the same commit that runs our tests.
